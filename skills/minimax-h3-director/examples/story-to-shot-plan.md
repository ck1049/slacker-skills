Prompt: “Cinematic realistic night-to-dawn sequence in a rain-soaked neon city. A tired courier with a red satchel runs through heavy rain, keeping a yellow envelope protected inside his dark coat. Start with a lateral tracking medium shot matching his pace. He reaches an awning, stops, and checks the address in a close-up with a slow push-in; wet gloves, red satchel, and yellow envelope remain consistent.
